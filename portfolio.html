Academic background

Pulse-like Effects of Near-fault Earthquakes on Reinforced Concrete Buildings in Mandalay, Myanmar

M.Eng thesis

Major Advisor: Dr. Teraphan Ornthammarath, Mahidol University · Chairman: Dr. Chatpan Chintanapakdee, Chulalongkorn University

Research theme
The relation between the pulse period (Tp) of near-fault ground motions and the fundamental period (T1) of the structure, studied across 4-, 10- and 16-storey buildings. Near-fault response is also compared with far-field earthquakes.
Ground motions
Two suites, near-fault (with pulse) and far-field (without pulse), each with 7 pairs selected for target intensity, site condition and seismotectonic setting. Linear scaling to the target Uniform Hazard Spectrum (UHS).
Pulse classification
Quantitative classification algorithm (Shahi & Baker, 2014) to identify significant pulses in the near-fault records.
Buildings
Three RC special moment-resisting frames (4, 10 and 16 storeys) designed to ASCE 7 and local codes.
Modelling & analysis
Full nonlinear 3D models in SeismoStruct; 21 nonlinear response history analyses; MATLAB post-processing of performance objectives, inter-storey drift ratio and element forces.

Performance-based seismic design

Forum

PBSD evaluation

Mandaluyong City, Philippines · Four 35-storey residential towers (~180 m) on a common podium

Structural system
Gravity loads carried by beams and slabs to the RC core wall and columns. Lateral forces resisted mainly by the RC central core wall and special moment frames.
Foundation
Mat foundation under the core walls; isolated footings under columns.
Procedure
Girders and coupling beams as frame elements with lumped shear/moment hinges at the ends; columns as fibre-section frames; shear walls as fibre-section shells with confined and unconfined concrete. Full nonlinear model analysed in Perform3D with 11 pairs of ground motions.
Results
Performance objectives and displacements checked against the Tall Building Initiative (TBI) criteria. Equivalent linearisation of time-history forces for foundation design, followed by diaphragm seismic design for in-plane slab capacity.

Performance-based seismic design

District M

PBSD evaluation

Mandaluyong City, Philippines · Residential floors 7–41 on a six-level common podium

Structural system
Non-prestressed RC flat slabs for gravity. Lateral forces resisted by coupled special RC shear walls at the core, plus an outrigger system along the Y axis: two levels of RC outrigger walls coupling the shear walls and outrigger columns.
Foundation
Cast-in-place bored piles.
